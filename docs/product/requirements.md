# Product Requirements

## Current priorities

- Provide reliable fixed-time and randomized reminder experiences, with randomized daily volume controlled by administrators.
- Provide native iOS and Android affirmation widgets that display the latest notification affirmation when reminders are enabled and otherwise rotate an eligible affirmation every three hours.
- Apply the user's selected affirmation topics to every affirmation surface, including the app, notifications, and widgets.
- Deliver an Affirmations + Settings experience with configurable affirmations; retain Calendar behind a disabled visibility flag:
  - `docs/product/affirmations-settings-feature-spec.md`
  - `docs/product/affirmations-screen-spec.md`
  - `docs/product/topic-selection-screen-spec.md`
  - `docs/product/background-selection-screen-spec.md`
- Persist liked affirmations now so they can power a future My Favorites section.
- Define product analog schema for future catalog rollout.
- Define commerce and content-delivery contracts before implementation.
